# Product Definition

## Product

CareCompass AI is a phone-first coordination layer that turns care documents into source-linked draft actions which a patient or caregiver reviews before activation.

## Initial user

A patient or family caregiver managing recovery after hospital discharge, initially focused on rehabilitation and home-care instructions.

## V1 job to be done

> Upload a discharge, rehabilitation or care document, review a structured 14-day action plan and see confirmed actions on Today.

## V1 outcomes

- Reduce the effort required to understand and organise care instructions.
- Make important actions traceable to source material.
- Surface ambiguity instead of inventing missing details.
- Help families prepare questions for clinicians.

## V1 capabilities

- Account and Care Space creation
- PDF/image capture and secure upload
- Structured extraction and plain-language summary
- Source-linked draft actions and uncertainties
- Review, edit, remove, clarify and confirm controls
- Today screen for confirmed actions
- Printable action-plan export

## Excluded claims and capabilities

V1 does not diagnose, prescribe, change medication instructions, adapt treatment, autonomously triage emergencies or replace professional judgement. Wearables, clinician decision support and insurance workflows are outside V1.

## Primary success question

Can CareCompass faithfully transform a difficult care document into a useful, traceable plan without inventing material information?
