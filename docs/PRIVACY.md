# Privacy Principles

This document describes engineering principles and is not a final privacy notice or legal opinion.

## Principles

- Collect only information required for a defined user purpose.
- Explain each use at or before collection.
- Request granular consent where consent is the applicable basis.
- Limit access by role and Care Space membership.
- Define retention before collecting data.
- Support access, correction, export and deletion.
- Keep production information out of development and testing.
- Review every external processor and data location.

## Pre-beta requirements

- Approved data inventory and processing purposes
- User-facing privacy notice
- Consent and permission records
- Retention and deletion schedule
- Processor and cross-border transfer review
- Data-protection impact assessment where appropriate
- Incident-response and notification process
- Tested account and Care Space deletion

## AI processing

Documents sent for AI processing must use an approved server-side integration and documented retention controls. Provider configuration and contracts must be reviewed for each production jurisdiction. Users must be told when AI is used and where human confirmation is required.
